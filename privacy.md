# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

"Saffron: Expenses tracker" ("the app") is a personal finance tracker developed by Yevhen Shevchenko ("I", "me").
This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
Everything you enter stays on your device. The only information that
leaves your device is anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off.

## Data you enter

Transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, savings goals, payments and
subscriptions are stored only in a local database on your device.
They are never sent to me or to any third party.

## Settings

Your preferences (language, currency, date and time formats) are stored
locally on your device.

## Backups

When you create a backup, the app saves a file on your device.
The file leaves your device only if you choose to share it
(for example, to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email) using your device's
share menu. In that case the service you choose handles the file under
its own privacy policy.

Your device's own system backup (such as an iCloud backup of your iPhone)
may include the app's data according to your device settings.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service I use to find and fix bugs.
A report contains the error message and code location, the app version,
the device model and operating system version.
It does not contain your transactions, amounts, notes, account or category
names, and it is not linked to your identity. IP addresses are not stored.
Reports are stored in the EU and are deleted automatically
after a limited retention period.

You can turn crash reports off at any time in Settings.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, does not
track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

## Internet access

The app works offline. It uses the internet only to send crash reports
(if enabled). Your financial data is never sent over the internet.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from them.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all its data from your device.
Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected and can be deleted by you.
